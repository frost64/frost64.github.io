Hello World: Why I Built This Site

Sep 01, 2026 · #dev · #learning

I've wanted a small place on the internet that I completely control — somewhere to collect my projects, write down what I'm learning, and share ideas that are too detailed for a short social post.

Why a personal website?

Social platforms change. A personal website is different. The layout, content, URLs, and archives belong to me, and I can keep improving them as my work evolves.

What I'll write about

Most posts will be about software development, products I'm building, problems I've run into, and lessons that may be useful to someone solving a similar problem.

What comes next

For now, the goal is simple: keep building and publish what I learn.
